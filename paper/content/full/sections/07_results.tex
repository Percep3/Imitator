\section{Results}

\subsection{Isolated-sign transcription}

The retrained models match 44/320 clips (13.75\%) on fold~7 and 35/320
(10.94\%) on fold~8. Mean token edit similarity is 18.28\% and 14.38\%,
respectively. Both emit EOS on every clip, but the output contains more tokens
than the reference on 249/320 clips (77.81\%) in fold~7 and 253/320 (79.06\%)
in fold~8. On the synthetic sequence benchmark,
the same checkpoints reach 12.28\% and 14.96\% strict accuracy, respectively.
These exploratory results are separate from the registered confirmation below.

\subsection{Transcription of two to eight signs}

On development folds 4--6, \ImitatorEOne{} obtains 18.1, 19.8, and 16.9\% strict
accuracy, versus 2.2, 2.3, and 1.8\% for the initial autoregressive model. This
baseline paired a two-layer causal Transformer decoder (128 hidden units, four
attention heads, and a 512-unit feed-forward layer) with tied token input and
output embeddings. Its visual encoder used an ST-GCN, temporal convolutions, and
a Transformer, but lacked global temporal positional encoding. The decoder used
Gemma's full 262,400-token vocabulary and teacher-forced cross-entropy.  Strict
sequence accuracy improves by roughly an order of magnitude, and the fraction
of sequences containing at least one error remains high.  The positional
intervention raises pairwise label-order agreement from near chance to about
0.99.

Figure~\ref{fig:keypoint-interpretability} shows that the left hand contributes
28.9--41.6\% of normalized attribution and causes the largest ablation loss for
the central ``a'' and ``perf'' decisions (7.92 and 15.77 log-probability points).
Face keypoints also receive substantial local attribution, whereas their ablation
effect is smaller; this distinction indicates sensitivity without equivalent
causal necessity.  Pose matters mainly for ``perf,'' and the final ``ume'' subtoken
is comparatively insensitive to all four coarse removals.  The agreement between
hand saliency and hand ablation supports a manually grounded decision in this
example, but one selected sequence cannot establish a dataset-wide anatomy claim.

\subsection{Exploratory token correction without sentence context}

Table~\ref{tab:gemma-tolerance} shows that \Gemma{} preserves exact content in
95\% of clean rows.  Exact accuracy is zero before and after correction at each
tested nonzero substitution rate, and chrF decreases.  Even the lowest nonzero
nominal rate yields at least one substitution per label, limiting what this
experiment can establish about milder corruption.
