\section{Conclusion}

\system{} predicts \Gemma{} IDs directly from sign motion.  This interface makes
it possible to score one isolated sign and then test the additional demands of
ordered transcription.  On synthetic streams of two to eight LSA64 signs, the
original checkpoints improve strict transcription on held-out signers.  The
exploratory correction audit found no exact recovery at the tested nonzero rates
for isolated labels.  Natural sentence translation and correction remain open.
