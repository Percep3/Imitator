\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Sign encoders and signer generalization.}
Spatial--temporal graph convolutional networks provide a natural representation of
skeleton motion \citep{yan2018stgcn}.  Recent skeleton-based continuous sign
recognition studies combine joint groups, motion streams, temporal lift or
multi-scale features, while emphasizing limited signer diversity as a major
generalization bottleneck
\citep{min2025skeleton,nam2026liftsign,wang2025stnet}.  Manual and non-manual cues,
handshape-aware boundaries, and feature-attention mechanisms further show that
hands, face, body, and dynamics carry complementary information
\citep{harrouch2026manual,zhao2026multimodal,yenisari2025multifeature}.  These gains
must be balanced against overfitting in small Transformer datasets
\citep{huamaniLessIsMore}.  Our visual front end follows this skeleton tradition,
but the evaluated output is a sequence of LLM token IDs rather than a gloss-class
sequence.

\paragraph{Connecting signs to language models.}
Sign2GPT couples sign representations to an LLM through pseudo-gloss pretraining,
SignLLM constructs discrete hierarchical sign tokens, and FLa-LLM factorizes visual
and language learning so that linguistic priors do not overwhelm visual grounding
\citep{wong2024sign2gpt,gong2024signllm,chen2024flallm}.  A low-resource pipeline
has also combined ST-GCN recognition with a distilled language generator
\citep{pai2026tsl}.  These systems motivate separating visual recognition from
language realization, but differ in what crosses the interface.  \system{} emits
real tokenizer IDs that can be inspected and decoded without training a learned
adapter inside the LLM.

\paragraph{Continuous and discrete LLM interfaces.}
Related speech systems learn continuous prompts or use CTC posteriors to form
weighted LLM-token embeddings \citep{deng2025wav2prompt,ma2025legoslm}.  Continuous
interfaces can retain uncertainty, whereas exact IDs are auditable and compatible
with an unmodified text API.  CTC remains a standard monotonic alignment objective
\citep{graves2006ctc} and has been used in skeleton CSLR and joint sign translation
\citep{min2025skeleton,nam2026liftsign,camgoz2020signtransformers,tan2025textctc}.
We tested token-level CTC as an auxiliary development objective, but it did not
improve the selected autoregressive model.  Our final interface is therefore a
greedily decoded causal token sequence.
