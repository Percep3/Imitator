\section{Data and Protocol}

\subsection{Controlled sign streams}

LSA64 contains 3,200 videos of 64 Argentinian Sign Language signs, performed five
times by each of ten subjects \citep{ronchetti2016lsa64}.  We extract and normalize
111 two-dimensional upper-body, hand, and facial keypoints per frame.  Each model
example concatenates two to eight isolated clips, optionally separated by short
zero-valued neutral gaps.  The target is the concatenation of the \Gemma{}
tokenizations of the corresponding Spanish labels.

The sign order is sampled randomly.  Consequently, \Gemma{} cannot use a natural
sentence prior to guess the next sign, and any recovered order must come from the
video.  Conversely, these examples contain neither natural syntax nor
coarticulation.  We call the task \emph{controlled token transcription}, not
continuous sign-language translation.

\subsection{Signer-disjoint evaluation}

Each LOSO fold excludes one outer-test signer, uses a second signer for inner
validation, and trains on the remaining signers.  Folds 1--6 are development;
folds 7 and 8 are prospective confirmation; folds 9 and 10 remain reserved.  The
frozen E1 configuration uses seed 23, 2,048 synthetic training examples per epoch,
512 deterministic inner-validation examples, 30 epochs, and 896 deterministic
outer examples.  The checkpoint is selected by inner-validation edit similarity,
then strict accuracy.  Outer results never select a variant or epoch.

Only one training seed and two confirmatory signers were admitted under the compute
budget.  We therefore estimate sample uncertainty but do not claim to estimate
between-seed variability or population-level signer heterogeneity.

\subsection{Pre-registered questions}

The prospective protocol asks whether E1 beats a historical affine CIF system on
paired strict accuracy (H1), retains at least 11.9\% strict accuracy independently
on each new signer (H2), and demonstrates visually grounded ordering through both
pairwise order accuracy and a gold-boundary segment-permutation intervention (H3).
CIF is not a proposed final architecture: it is retained only as the pre-registered
historical comparator and signer-clean source initialization.
